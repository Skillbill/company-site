Our Team

Skillbill people are software developers. Thanks to their skills, they are able to cover many areas, from server software to complex user interfaces, mobile applications, mission critical field control software, streaming and multimedia applications and tools. Software is a key element of their professional interests. Participating in conferences and attending events, taking time for study and maintaining a constant exchange of support, we invest in technical skills to keep our team updated on the latest technologies and to make sure that colleagues can build successful careers.

We are always busy looking for the best languages, frameworks, skills, methodologies, and tools that allow us to develop better software. For Skillbill, better software means software with fewer problems, easy to maintain, and delivering top-class performance.
