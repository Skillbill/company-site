Some weeks ago the Marketing guy asked me for our logo in a vector format. We had several versions, each different from the others (and yes, a version with colors swapped by mistake!).

As a developer, like everyone here in Skillbill, I decided to produce the image by writing a program. I said the reason was to have a new version of the logo that is versioned and easier to manage... the true reason is that I love coding and graphical libraries/tools (never heard of [OpenScad](http://openscad.org/) ?!).

I created the logo in JS with the [Paper.js library](http://paperjs.org/).

Why not make an animation as well? :)
